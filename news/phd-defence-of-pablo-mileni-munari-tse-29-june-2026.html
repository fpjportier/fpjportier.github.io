PhD Defence of Pablo Mileni Munari (TSE, 29 June 2026)

2026-09-03

I had the chance to be a referee member of the jury for Pablo Mileni Munari’s defence that took place in Toulouse on 29 June 2026. Very good thesis that centers on his job market paper “Beyond Skills: Firms, Automation and Wage Inequality,” which develops a general equilibrium model showing how firm-level heterogeneity in automation adoption—not just worker skills—drives wage dispersion between similar workers. Kudos to Pablo and his adviser Fabrice Collard. Other members of the Jury were Eugenia Gonzalez-Aguado, Alexander Guembel and Charles Brendon.
