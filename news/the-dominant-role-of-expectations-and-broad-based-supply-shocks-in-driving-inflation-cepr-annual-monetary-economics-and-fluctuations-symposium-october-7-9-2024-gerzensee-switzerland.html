The Dominant Role of Expectations and Broad-Based Supply Shocks in Driving Inflation, CEPR Annual Monetary Economics and Fluctuations Symposium, October 7-9 , 2024, Gerzensee, Switzerland

2025-03-25

I attended the annual CEPR MEF Symposium and presented on October 7-9 2024 and presented a paper with Paul Beaudry and Sev Hou, “The Dominant Role of Expectations and Broad-Based Supply Shocks in Driving Inflation“.

Thanks to Refet S. Gürkaynak for organizing this.
