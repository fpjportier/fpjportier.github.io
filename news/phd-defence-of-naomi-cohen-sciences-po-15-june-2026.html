PhD Defence of Naomi Cohen (Sciences Po, 15 June 2026)

2026-09-03

I had the great pleasure to be a member of the jury for Naomi Cohen’s defence that took place in Paris on 15 June 2026. Very interesting thesis examining how international risk-sharing and financial market integration redistribute risk across households and countries in the eurozone. Naomi’s main paper uses microeconomic household portfolio data and cross-border equity data to show significant disparities in financial participation within countries and persistent asymmetries in portfolio diversification between them. Kudos to Naomi and her adviser Xavier Ragot. Other members of the jury were Stéphane Auray, Kenza Benhima, Nicolas Coeurdacier, and Evi Pappà.
