10th AMSE BDF Workshop in Macroeconomics (Aix-en-Provence, 18-19 June 2026)

2026-09-03

I have been lucky enough to be invited once more to the wonderful workshop organised by the Aix-Marseille School of Economics and the Banque de France. This year, because of the Rugby Top 14 semi-finals taking place in Marseille, the workshop was in Aix. You could not find more devoted organisers than Laurent Ferrara, Céline Poilly, Charles Serfaty and Daniele Siena (during the day and after). It was a pleasure once more, and I had the chance to be there with my colleague Morten Ravn. I presented my ongoing research with Paul Beaudry and Sev Hou “Revisiting Wage-Price Spirals”.

The full program of the conference is below.

Thursday, June 18

  • 14:00 — Ugo Panizza (Geneva Graduate Institute), The Sovereign Greenium: Big Promise but Small Price Effect. Discussant: Erica Perego (Catholic University of Milan)
  • 14:50 — Keynote Speech: Martin Uribe (Columbia University), The Effects of U.S. Import Tariff Shocks
  • 15:50 — Coffee break
  • 16:20 — Axelle Ferrière (Sciences Po Paris), Consumption Taxes and Aggregate Demand. Discussant: Aurélien Eyquem (University of Lausanne)
