XXVII Workshop on Dynamic Macroeconomics, July 2-4 , 2024, Pazo de Soutomaior, Vigo, Spain

2025-03-25

With a group of good friends, (Tim Kehoe (University of Minnesota), Árpád Ábráhám (Bristol), Jaime Alonso-Carrera (Universidade de Vigo), Omar Licandro (University of Nottingham), Sergio Feijoo Moreira (Bristol), Finn Kydland (UCSB), Nick Pretnar (UCSDB)  and J. Víctor Ríos-Rull (University of Pensylvania)), I have organized the XXVIIth edition of the Vigo Workshop on Dynamic Macroeconomics.  (Jaime is  the real organiser of the workshop). 

The workshop takes place in a fantastic location. It is the perfect combination of good economics (a lot of) and fun (a lot of). Presentation are done by PhD students.

As far as economics is conerned, here was the program:

Tuesday, 2 July

9:30-10:30: Pablo Mileni Munari (Toulouse School of Economics) “Anatomy of the pass-through of productivity shocks”

10:30-11:30: Marta García Rodríguez (Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona) “The Effect of Non- technological News Shocks on Unemployment Fluctuations: The Case of Europe”

12:00-13:00: Gabriele Lucchetti (University of Nottingham) “Skills, Distortions, and the Labor Market Outcomes of Immigrants across Space”

13:00-14:00: Samuel M. Marshall (University of Warwick) “Labor Market Power in Sub-Saharan Africa: The Role of Small Firms, Self-Employment, and Migration”

15:30-16:30: Alejandro Rábano (Universitat Pompeu Fabra) “Firms and Big Data: Adoption, Use and Impacts”

16:30-17:30: Jacob T. Hess (Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona) “Invisible Assets, Visible Growth: Multinationals, Intangibles and the Wage Skill Premium in Spain”

18:00-19:00: Cristian Espinosa (University College London) “From protective to retaliatory tariffs: Identifying trade elasticities”

Wednesday, 3 July

9:30-10:30: Miquel Oliver i Vert (University of Nottingham) “Customer base and the advertising sector”

10:30-11:30: Riccardo Silvestrini (Erasmus University Rotterdam) “Market Power Increase and Sectoral Heterogeneity: the Role of e-Commerce Platforms” 11:30-12:00: Break

12:00-13:00: Selma Malmberg (Le Mans University & CEPREMAP) “Income tax fluctuations and uncertainty in France”

13:00-14:00: Matthias Hänsel (Stockholm School of Economics) “Idiosyncratic Risk, Government Debt and Inflation”

Thursday, 4 July

9:30-10:30: Shane Mahen (University of Cambridge) “The Clean Transition in a Network Economy”

10:30-11:30: Yann Perdereau (Paris School of Economics) “Geography versus Income: the Heterogeneous Effects of Carbon Taxation”

12:00-13:00: Anastasis Koufakis (Universidad Carlos III de Madrid) “Wealth-Age Dynamics, the Housing Cycle and the Recession in Spain”

13:00-14:00: Juan Castellanos (European University Institute, Florence) “The aggregate and distributional implications of credit shocks on housing and rental markets”
