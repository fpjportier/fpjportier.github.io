Seminar in Cambridge, October 29, 2012

2012-11-09

I presented my joint paper with Paul Beaudry “A Gains from Trade Approach to Macroeconomic Fluctuations” in a seminar at the department of economics of Cambridge University last Monday. Thanks to Pontus Rendahl for the invitation.

Anecdotically, I gave the seminar in a room where is presented one copy of the famous MONIAC (Monetary National Income Analogue Computer) also known as the Phillips Hydraulic Computer. Cool machine! (see photo on the left)
