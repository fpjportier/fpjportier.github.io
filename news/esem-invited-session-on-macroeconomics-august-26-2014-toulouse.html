ESEM Invited Session on Macroeconomics, August 26, 2014, Toulouse

2014-08-26

 I am today (10:45, Amphi Boyer) chairing the ESEM Invited Session on Macroeconomics here in Toulouse. The two speakers are Emmanuel Farhi and Kjetil Storesletten. The two presented papers will be:

 

A THEORY OF MACROPRUDENTIAL POLICIES IN THE PRESENCE OF NOMINAL RIGIDITIES

Presented by Emmanuel Farhi, Harvard University

OPTIMAL TAXATION WITH HUMAN CAPITAL AND PARTIAL INSURANCE
Presented by Kjetil Storesletten, University of Oslo
