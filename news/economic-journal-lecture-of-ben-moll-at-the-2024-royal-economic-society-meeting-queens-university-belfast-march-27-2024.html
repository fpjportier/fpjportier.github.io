Economic Journal Lecture of Ben Moll at the 2024 Royal Economic Society Meeting, Queen’s University, Belfast, March 27, 2024.

2024-06-02

I had the chance to chair the Economic Journal Lecture of Ben Moll (LSE) at the 2024 Royal Economic Society Meeting at the Queen’s University in Belfast. The title of Ben’s talk was Heterogeneous Agent Macroeconomics: Eight Lessons and a Challenge. The lecture will be published in the Economic Journal, and it will be a must read!
