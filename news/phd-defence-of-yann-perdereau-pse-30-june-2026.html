PhD Defence of Yann Perdereau (PSE, 30 June 2026)

2026-09-03

I was lucky enough to be a referee member of the jury for Yann Perdereau’s defence that took place in Paris on 30 June 2026. Very cool thesis on heterogeneous-agent macroeconomic models applied to monetary policy, climate, and fiscal policy. Kudos to Yann and his adviser Gilles Saint Paul. Other members of the jury were Patrick Fève, Tobias Broer, Axelle Ferrière, and Gianluca Violante.
