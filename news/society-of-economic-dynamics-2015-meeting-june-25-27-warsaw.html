Society of Economic Dynamics 2015 Meeting, June 25-27, Warsaw

2015-10-24

I have attended the 2015 SED meeting in Warsaw. As I write every year, as good as always.  I presented my  paper with Paul Beaudry and Dana Galicia “Reconciling Hayek’s and Keynes’ views of recessions” in a session ” Understanding Aggregate Demand Shortages” that was organized by Alp Simsek.
