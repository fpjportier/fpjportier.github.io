8th Aix-Marseille School of Economics — Banque de France Macro Workshop, Marseille June 21, 2024

2025-03-25

I have been lucky enough to be invited (once more, and again with my colleague Morten Ravn) to the Aix-Marseille School of Economics — Banque de France Macro Workshop. Thanks to the organizers Laurent Ferrara, Céline Poilly, Charles Serfaty, Daniele Siena.

I discussed Pierre Mabille (INSEAD) paper:
Aggregate Precautionary Savings Motives.
