Explore Econ 2024, UCL, May 23, 2024.

2024-06-02

I attended (unfortunately not in extenso) the 2024 Explore Econ conference at UCL. Explore Econ is the annual undergraduate student research conference, organised by the Centre for Teaching and Learning Economics (CTaLE) in the Department of Economics, showcasing undergrad stiudents economics research both in and outside the curriculum.

BSc students are invited to submit a paper or poster entry. All submissions are reviewed by the Scientific Committee, with the best entries shortlisted for peer review. The shortlisted entries are be showcased online for a second round of peer shortlisting, with the best entries awarded prizes and invited to present at the Explore Econ conference in May.

The best entries are also be submitted for publication in the Economic Tribune’s Journal of Economics.

I discussed Alessandro Guanieri ‘s (now grad student in(Oxford) very interesting paper “Monetary Policy, Portfolio Heterogeneity, and the College Wealth Gap“, published in the UCL Journal of Economics. The UCL Journal of Economics is the official peer-reviewed, student-run journal of the UCL Department of Economics. In line with UCL’s philosophy of a research focused approach to education, it aims to provide a platform for undergraduate economists at UCL and worldwide to showcase their rigorous economic research, regardless of background or previous experience.
