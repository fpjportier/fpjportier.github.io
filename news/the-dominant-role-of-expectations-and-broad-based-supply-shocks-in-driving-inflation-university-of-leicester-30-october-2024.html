The Dominant Role of Expectations and Broad-Based Supply Shocks in Driving Inflation, University of Leicester, 30 October 2024

2025-03-25

I gave a seminar at University of Leicester on 30 October 2024 and presented a paper with Paul Beaudry and Sev Hou, “The Dominant Role of Expectations and Broad-Based Supply Shocks in Driving Inflation“.

Thanks to Deborah Gefang for organizing my visit. I had a nice talk and diner with Guillaume Wilemme and my 30 years-old (!) friend Omar Licandro. I also talk to  Tim Obermeier who was a UCL pot doc on the market a few years ago.
