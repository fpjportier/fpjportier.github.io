The Dominant Role of Expectations and Broad-Based Supply Shocks in Driving Inflation, Birkbeck College, June 14, 2024

2025-03-25

I gave a seminar at Birkbeck College (shortest seminar trip I could do, about 5mn walking from my office) on June 14 2024 and presented a paper with Paul Beaudry and Sev Hou, “The Dominant Role of Expectations and Broad-Based Supply Shocks in Driving Inflation“.

Thanks to Daniel Kaliski for organizing my visit. It is always a pleasure to discuss economics with Yunus Aksoy, Pedro Gomes and Stephen Wright.
