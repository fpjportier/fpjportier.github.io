Financial Markets and the Real Economy Conference, May 4 and 5, 2013, Venice

2013-05-06

Marija Vukotic (Warwick) and my former colleague in Toulouse (now in Warwick) Roberto Pancrazi organized a great conference in Venice last weekend. Below is the program. I discussed  a paper by Marija, Roberto and Hernan D. Seoane (from Carlos III, Madrid) entitled “Sovereign Debt Crisis and Private Credit”. Here is my discussion. Two of my colleagues (or future colleagues), Christian Hellwig and Robert Ulbricht, were presenting.
