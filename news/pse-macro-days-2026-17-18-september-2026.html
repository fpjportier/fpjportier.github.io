PSE Macro Days 2026, 17-18 September 2026

2026-09-15

I have the chance to participate in the PSE Macro Days on the 17 and 18 of September 2026 in Paris. I will present recent advances of my work on wage-price spirals with Sev Hou and Paul Beaudry (we have been working hard this summer). It is always a pleasure to come back to my alma mater. Thanks to the organisers Tobias Broer, Juan Carluccio, Edouard Challe, Riccardo Cioffi and Gilles Saint-Paul for including us in the program. The program of these two days is below.
