This is the page of MRes–Macro I course

University College London

 

2025-2026

ECON 0107 is a macroeconomic course for MRes students. I am teaching that course with Łukasz Rachel, Morten Ravn and Vincent Sterk. (first term: 5 weeks taught by me, then 5 weeks taught by Lukasz Rachel, second term: 5 weeks taught by Vincent Sterk and 5 weeks by Morten Ravn)

The first part of the first semester will be mostly about teaching the language of modern macroeconomics.

I follow closely Lars Ljungqvist and Thomas Sargent textbook Recursive Macroeconomic Theory, Cambridge, Mass.: MIT Press, fourth edition.
